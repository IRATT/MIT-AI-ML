Course case · Modules 9–10

Production optimization

$2,316modeled weekly contribution

Choose a production mix under labor and capacity constraints. Linear optimization translates margins and resource limits into a feasible schedule; sensitivity analysis identifies where extra capacity could create value.

Key insight: invest where a binding constraint limits the objective, and revisit the recommendation when conditions change.

Network logistics

$541,000minimum cost in the course model

Allocate flows through a supply and disposal network while respecting supply limits, depot capacities and landfill requirements. The Gurobipy model makes the feasible routes and cost trade-offs explicit.

Key insight: a recommendation must reconcile with the executed flow table and the constraints it satisfies.

Clinical AI

Clinician-ledfrom conversation to structured records

Use speech recognition and NLP to draft structured clinical documentation in PAN Enterprise. Practitioners review, correct and approve the record; governed data can then support operational analytics.

Evaluation: compare documentation time, completeness and correction rates with a baseline before claiming benefit.

Alarm intelligence

Observe. Prove.accountable AI for critical infrastructure

Apply contextual recommendations to alarm triage across power and data-center operations. A staged approach combines local resilience, clear operator authority and evaluation before any bounded automation.
