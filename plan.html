The structure translates a 24-module course into a navigable body of evidence for technical and executive audiences.

Purpose and audience

Demonstrate the ability to connect analytics with operational decisions. Technical reviewers can inspect models and assumptions; business leaders can see the recommendation, governance and implementation implications.

Implementation sequence

1 / Verify and inventory

Review instructions, course completion, module topics, final grades and submitted evidence.

2 / Curate and explain

Write concise learner summaries; prepare reviewed model extracts; separate course outcomes from prospective applications and retain original papers privately.

3 / Build a coherent experience

Create responsive pages, a searchable module catalogue, consistent evidence links and accessible navigation.

4 / Validate and publish

Reconcile grades, inspect numerical claims, check internal links, test filters and publish through GitHub Pages.

5 / Extend with evidence

Add new independent projects with a problem statement, reproducible method, measured result, limitations and a clear connection to the learning.

Recommended next additions

  • Export any remaining personally owned Colab notebooks, with permissions and reproducibility checked before inclusion.
  • Develop independent, reproducible versions of selected Codio exercises; label these as later projects rather than original course submissions.
  • Add measured pilot outcomes to the clinical AI and alarm-management studies when suitable evidence is available for public release.
  • Review links and artifact versions whenever new work is added. Preserve corrections in version history.

Definition of a strong project entry

State the decision and constraints, identify the data and method, show the supported result, explain limitations, provide readable evidence and identify what would be required for deployment.

Design direction

Restrained navy, cream and teal. Strong editorial typography. Clear evidence links. Readable without a login.
