The capacity insight

ResourceUsed / availableInterpretation
Frame manufacturing40 / 40 hoursBinding
Finishing60 / 60 hoursBinding
Stretching69.8 / 80 hours10.2 hours slack

Frame manufacturing and finishing are binding. Stretching has 10.2 hours of unused capacity, so additional stretching capacity has no marginal value at the current solution. Coffee-table and end-table production also reaches the stated upper limits.

From model to management action

Adopt the modeled schedule only within the case assumptions, investigate capacity where it can relax a binding limit, and re-solve when demand, costs or process times change. A shadow price is local to a sensitivity range; it is not an unlimited business case for expansion.
