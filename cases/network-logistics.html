Python · Network optimization

A transparent route to least-cost logistics.

A Gurobipy network-flow model with supply limits, depot capacities and landfill requirements.

Submitted notebook + interpretation

The decision

Route waste from two supply centers through a network of four transfer depots and six landfill destinations at minimum transportation cost. The route selection must respect supply limits, required deliveries and depot throughput.

Model structure

  • Decision variables: nonnegative flow on each of 29 permitted arcs.
  • Objective: minimize the sum of flow multiplied by route unit cost.
  • Supply limits: each center’s outbound total must remain within capacity.
  • Conservation: each depot’s incoming and outgoing flow must balance.
  • Destination requirements: each landfill receives its specified amount.
  • Throughput: each depot respects its handling limit.
Recorded solver result

The submitted Gurobipy notebook reports an optimal objective of $541,000 for 29 variables and 16 constraints.

Evidence quality

The table above follows the executed notebook output. The written submission transcribes the Staten Island destination as C4; the notebook correctly shows C3. The original written artifact is retained privately, unchanged, and the discrepancy is made explicit here.

The public notebook preserves source and static outputs with Colab interface metadata removed. It uses the course-provided model and has not been rerun in the original Gurobi environment as part of publication. An independent SciPy formulation reproduces the $541,000 objective.
