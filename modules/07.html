Module 07 / Prediction

Logistic Regression

Connect classification errors to business value.

Logistic regressionConfusion matrixThresholds

The focus

Applied logistic regression to loan repayment and interpreted the consequences of classification errors. The executive memo considered both predictive quality and a lending decision.

My work

The submitted LendingClub case memo reported approximately 77% test accuracy using a 70/30 split. The assignment scenario estimated profit increasing from about $5.6 million to $6.1 million. These are course-case estimates, not realized client results.

The decision-making insight

Accuracy alone is insufficient. False approvals and false denials carry different costs, so thresholds and confusion-matrix outcomes matter.

From learning to application

Decision support: pair classification metrics with explicit business costs and human review.

Application ideas describe potential uses of the learning; they are not claims of measured deployment outcomes.
