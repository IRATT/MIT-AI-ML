Optimization: Part Five

Design the least-cost route through a network.

PythonGurobipyNetwork flow

The focus

Worked through a landfill logistics model with supply centers, transfer depots and destination requirements. The submitted notebook contains the formulation and executed solver output.

My work

The notebook reports a minimum modeled transportation cost of $541,000, using 29 flow variables and 16 constraints. The formulation enforces center limits, landfill requirements, depot conservation and depot throughput limits.

The decision-making insight

The executed flow table is the reference for this portfolio. It routes 80,000 units from Staten Island to C3; the written dashboard labels that destination C4 in a transcription error.

From learning to application

Supply-chain and infrastructure planning: optimize flows while preserving capacity and conservation constraints.

Application ideas describe potential uses of the learning; they are not claims of measured deployment outcomes.
