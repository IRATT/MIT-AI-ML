Optimization: Part Two

Find the bottleneck before funding more capacity.

Product mixShadow pricesExecutive communication

The focus

Used the Craft Studio Furnishings case to connect an optimal production mix with resource utilization and a management recommendation.

My work

My submitted memo recommends 0 table chairs, 4 easy chairs, 20 love seats, 10 coffee tables and 10 end tables per week, for a modeled weekly contribution of $2,316. Frame manufacturing and finishing are fully utilized; stretching has spare capacity.

The decision-making insight

Additional capacity creates value only where it relaxes a binding limitation within the relevant sensitivity range. The recommendation must change when the inputs change.

From learning to application

Manufacturing and service operations: prioritize investments using marginal value, then re-solve the model.

Application ideas describe potential uses of the learning; they are not claims of measured deployment outcomes.
