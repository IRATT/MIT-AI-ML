Module 11 / Optimization

Optimization: Part Three

Combine demand prediction with pricing decisions.

Demand modelingNonlinear optimizationValidation

The focus

Built a demand model using current price, two lagged prices and seasonal terms, then used it to recommend an eight-week pricing schedule for an amusement-park case.

My work

The submitted analysis explains the negative current-price coefficient and positive lagged-price effects. It also proposes price-change limits, additional demand drivers and a controlled field experiment to evaluate actual value.

The decision-making insight

Optimized revenue estimated by the same model is not independent evidence of improvement. A prospective experiment is needed to test business impact.

From learning to application

Commercial planning: use forecast-driven optimization with price guardrails and ongoing recalibration.

Application ideas describe potential uses of the learning; they are not claims of measured deployment outcomes.
