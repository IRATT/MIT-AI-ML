Clustering

Turn heterogeneous customers into useful segments.

K-meansNormalizationUnsupervised learning

The focus

Studied clustering as a way to discover structure without labeled outcomes. Airline customer segmentation provided a practical setting for considering what makes a cluster meaningful to a business.

My work

Completed the K-means coding assignment and exercises on normalization, algorithm components and clustering tradeoffs. The work emphasized choosing comparable features and interpreting groups beyond their numerical labels.

The decision-making insight

Feature scaling can change the distance calculation and therefore the clusters. A statistically compact cluster still needs an operational interpretation.

From learning to application

Service design: segment usage patterns to tailor support, capacity and customer offerings.

Application ideas describe potential uses of the learning; they are not claims of measured deployment outcomes.
