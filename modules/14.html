The focus

Compared tree-based models with regression and used a park-attendance case to communicate nonlinear relationships.

My work

Completed the CART coding assignment and submitted an executive memo on temperature, time of day and humidity. The memo describes temperature as the strongest attendance driver and highlights daily peaks in the modeled relationships.

The decision-making insight

Partial-dependence patterns describe a fitted model. They should not automatically be interpreted as causal effects, especially when features are correlated.
