Neural Networks: Part One

Learn representations from complex behavior.

Neural networksActivation functionsFraud detection

The focus

Introduced neural networks and considered their suitability for structured and unstructured inputs. The written assignment explored social-network fraud as a changing behavioral problem.

My work

My fraud analysis proposed combining language, images, relationships and activity patterns, while examining false positives, evolving behavior, representative data and privacy.

The decision-making insight

An unusual behavior is not necessarily fraudulent. Thresholds, drift monitoring and a review process are essential to protect legitimate users.

From learning to application

Trust and safety: use learned representations to prioritize investigation with appropriate review.

Application ideas describe potential uses of the learning; they are not claims of measured deployment outcomes.
