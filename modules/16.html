The focus

Examined representation, measurement and automation bias through a facial-recognition analysis and a lending-model board memo.

My work

In the FairBank course case, both groups had a 16% overall error rate, yet false-denial rates were 30% and 18%. My memo recommended subgroup evaluation, better measures of repayment ability and ongoing monitoring.

The decision-making insight

Equal overall error rates can conceal materially different harms. Model evaluation must reflect the affected people and the consequence of each error.

From learning to application

AI governance: define subgroup checks, escalation rules and meaningful human accountability.

Application ideas describe potential uses of the learning; they are not claims of measured deployment outcomes.
