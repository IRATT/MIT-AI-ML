The focus

Explored recommendation models that infer preferences from user–item interactions. Archetypes and latent representations offer a way to work with sparse rating matrices.

My work

Completed the Jester joke-recommendation assignment and a music collaborative-filtering portfolio assignment in Codio. These exercises developed and evaluated predictions for missing user–item ratings.

The decision-making insight

Observed ratings are selective. Sparsity, cold-start users and popularity effects should shape the interpretation of recommendation quality.

From learning to application

Product personalization: recommend useful next actions while retaining exploration and user choice.

Application ideas describe potential uses of the learning; they are not claims of measured deployment outcomes.
