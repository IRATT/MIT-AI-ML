The focus

Used probability distributions to represent uncertain demand and connect predictive information to prescriptive decisions. The coursework moved from a single expected value toward the range of outcomes a manager must plan for.

My work

Completed the patient-booking exercise in Codio, applying predictive data to a prescriptive model. The original hosted notebook is not exportable; this page records the task, concepts and verified assessment result.

The decision-making insight

Capacity decisions should account for variability as well as average demand. Pooling can change the risk profile when demands are not perfectly correlated.

From learning to application

Clinic scheduling: compare booking policies against both utilization and service reliability.

Application ideas describe potential uses of the learning; they are not claims of measured deployment outcomes.
