Correlation

Understand what moves together—and why it matters.

The focus

Explored relationships between variables, including how covariance affects a combined portfolio and how correlation supports initial investigation of business data.

My work

Completed the coding assignment on analyzing data for a prescriptive model. The exercises connected measured relationships to portfolio and business decisions without treating association as proof of causation.

The decision-making insight

Two individually variable assets or workloads can produce a more stable combination when their movements differ. The benefit depends on the joint behavior.

From learning to application

Operations planning: examine whether loads, failures or demand streams peak together.

Application ideas describe potential uses of the learning; they are not claims of measured deployment outcomes.
