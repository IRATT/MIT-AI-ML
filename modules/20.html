Natural Language Processing: Part One

Distinguish contextual similarity from meaning.

Word embeddingsNLPSentiment analysis

The focus

Explored word representations and sentiment classification. A written analysis tested whether vector similarity distinguishes synonyms from antonyms.

My work

My embedding experiment found high/low similarity of 0.8511, compared with good/excellent at 0.8062. I also completed the IMDB sentiment-classification portfolio assignment in Codio.

The decision-making insight

Words with opposite meanings may appear in similar contexts and therefore have nearby vectors. A downstream task needs context beyond similarity alone.

From learning to application

Clinical and operational text: evaluate semantic errors before using extracted language in decisions.

Application ideas describe potential uses of the learning; they are not claims of measured deployment outcomes.
