Linear Regression: Part One

Explain a model in language a decision maker can use.

Multiple regressionR²Outlier review

The focus

Interpreted a house-price regression and examined the treatment of unusual observations. Two submitted analyses translated statistical output into recommendations and limitations.

My work

The housing memo discussed a supplied model with R² = 0.768 and construction quality, living area, garage area and basement area as predictors. The mortgage-points analysis recommended checking unusual records against their source before exclusion.

The decision-making insight

A good fit does not establish causation. Outlier thresholds are screening tools; unusual but valid cases may contain important information.

From learning to application

Property and operational forecasting: document variable meaning, uncertainty and data-cleaning decisions.
