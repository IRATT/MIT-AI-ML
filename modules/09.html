Optimization: Part One

Express a management decision as a solvable model.

Linear programmingExcel SolverConstraints

The focus

Formulated decision variables, objectives and constraints for advertising, production and coal procurement. Three assignments developed the translation from a business narrative to a mathematical model.

My work

The submitted coal model records a total supply cost of $73,267,500 and an average of about $59.81 per ton. Advertising coverage was expressed as explicit audience constraints. Reviewed numerical extracts are available below.

The decision-making insight

Units and constraint direction deserve as much attention as the objective. A solver optimizes the model it receives, including any formulation errors.

From learning to application

Procurement and capacity allocation: make tradeoffs and feasibility explicit before comparing options.

Application ideas describe potential uses of the learning; they are not claims of measured deployment outcomes.
