Module 15 / Prediction

Nonlinear Models for Regression and Classification: Ensemble Learning

Look beyond a strong headline accuracy.

XGBoostEnsemblesROC-AUC

The focus

Explored ensemble learning and assessed a loan-default classifier. The written submission considered feature importance and challenged a potentially misleading evaluation result.

My work

My analysis reported approximately 83.4% test accuracy, with FICO and installment among the leading features. It also flagged a reported 50% ROC-AUC for investigation and recommended using predicted probabilities and a confusion matrix.

The decision-making insight

A high accuracy score can hide failure on the minority class. Feature-importance scores describe the fitted model and do not prove causation.

From learning to application

Risk modeling: evaluate ranking, calibration and class-specific errors before choosing a decision threshold.

Application ideas describe potential uses of the learning; they are not claims of measured deployment outcomes.
