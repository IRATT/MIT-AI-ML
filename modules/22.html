The focus

Proposed incorporating third-party telematics into an insurance pricing model while preserving an understandable final decision process.

My work

My submitted paper recommends a separate behavioral-risk model feeding a simpler pricing layer, with checks for exposure, identity, sensor quality, consent, subgroup effects and drift.

The decision-making insight

Interpretability does not itself establish causality. A two-stage design still needs causal assumptions, appropriate validation and careful treatment of selection and confounding.

From learning to application

Decision architecture: separate rich representation learning from an auditable decision layer.

Application ideas describe potential uses of the learning; they are not claims of measured deployment outcomes.
