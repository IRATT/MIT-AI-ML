Use analytical tools to understand the situation, inform the decision and maximize economic value.

The journey moved from visualizing data and understanding its statistics through prediction and optimization to advanced models and deep learning. The wrap-up brought these methods together around a practical purpose: interpreting data within the context of a real business choice.

01Understand

Data visualization & statistics

What does the evidence say, and how uncertain is it?

I begin by examining how the data was collected, how it varies and which groups a headline result may conceal. Visualization and statistics help turn an apparent pattern into a question that can be investigated.

Methods: Visualization, probability distributions, correlation and clustering.

02Anticipate

Predictive modeling

What is likely to happen, and which prediction errors matter most?

Regression, classification and recommendation methods connect observed data to future outcomes. I evaluate generalization and choose metrics and thresholds that reflect the costs of different errors in the intended use.

Methods: Linear regression, logistic regression and collaborative filtering.

03Decide

Optimization

Which feasible action best serves the objective?

Optimization makes the decision explicit: what can change, what must be respected and what outcome is valuable. I check units, constraints and sensitivity so a recommendation remains connected to the operating conditions that support it.

Methods: Linear programming, sensitivity analysis, integer decisions and network flow.

04Refine

Advanced predictive modeling

Does a richer model improve the decision on new data?

Trees and ensembles extend the predictive toolkit to more complex relationships. I assess performance on unseen data, class imbalance and the limits of feature explanations before using a model to guide a consequential choice.

Methods: Classification and regression trees, random forests and XGBoost.

Deep learning

How can complex inputs support a reliable operational workflow?

Neural networks, transfer learning and NLP expand the kinds of information a system can use. I connect model outputs to task-specific validation, domain context and human review, particularly where a plausible output can still contain a consequential error.

Methods: Neural networks, TensorFlow/Keras, transfer learning, NLP and transformers.

My approach to applying the learning

Make the decision explicit.

A practical synthesis of the program, connected to the engineering and business context in which I work.

  1. Frame the decision

    Identify the decision owner, available actions, constraints and economic objective.

  2. Understand the evidence

    Examine data quality, variation, relevant populations and the limits of inference.

  3. Choose and validate

    Match the method to the question; evaluate predictions, feasibility, sensitivity and potential harm.

  4. Measure and improve

    Compare outcomes with a baseline, monitor performance and revise as conditions change.
