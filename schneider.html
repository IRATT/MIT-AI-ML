Data Center Foundations

Understand the systems behind uptime.

The Data Center Certified Associate Exam Learning Path connects availability, power, cooling, racks, cabling, security and infrastructure management to the business services they support.

APPLIED LEARNING

From concepts to practical choices.

Learning synthesis

Availability and recovery

Distinguish equipment reliability from service availability. Define when an outage starts and when the business service is actually usable again, then examine both failure prevention and restoration.

Restoring utility power is only one step in restoring a service.

Learning synthesis

Cooling where the load needs it

Relate rack layout, inlet conditions, humidity and airflow separation to hot spots. Compare room, row and rack cooling in the context of density and physical constraints.

Aggregate cooling capacity does not guarantee suitable rack inlet conditions.

Study-reference application

From infrastructure data to decisions

Use an accurate asset inventory and dependency model to relate power, environment and access alarms to affected services. The supplied study reference integrates modern DCIM and distributed-site monitoring.

A useful dashboard links a signal to its operational consequence.
